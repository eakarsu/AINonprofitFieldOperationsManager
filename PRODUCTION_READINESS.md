# Governed nonprofit field case
The durable path is `/api/governed-field-cases`. It covers intake/duplicate checks, eligibility and consent, restricted notes, approved service plans, owned referrals, volunteer onboarding/credentials/availability, shift confirmation/check-in/no-show recovery, supervision/hours, outcomes, safeguarding, restrictions, custody, offline sync, and closure.
Apply `backend/migrations/001_governed_field_case.sql` through deployment controls. SMS/email, mapping, background checks, donor CRM, grants/accounting, encrypted documents, and partner referrals remain unconfigured until consent, credentials, delivery/retry contracts, and reconciliation exist. Generated advice/agent routes are quarantined.
Urgent cases require staff escalation; consent, eligibility, duplicates, grant restrictions, staffing, messaging, and offline divergence fail closed. Coordinators, caseworkers, volunteers, donors, finance, safeguarding, administrators, and board auditors receive explicit roles and scoped evidence.
Configure from `.env.example`; run `node --test backend/governance/*.test.cjs` and `bash -n start.sh`. Startup no longer implicitly creates tables and the launcher does not mutate infrastructure.
